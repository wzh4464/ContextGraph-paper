\section{Published SWE-ContextBench reference results}
\label{sec:published-swectx}

Table~\ref{tab:published-swectx-lite} reproduces the resolved rates from
Table 4 of \citet{swecontext2026}. Supermemory leads the automatic
memory settings reported there; oracle summaries use known task relationships
to supply the relevant history. These published results provide context for
our Related-Lite experiments. Direct comparisons require matching task IDs,
backbone, agent, and attempt budget.

On the separate 376-task set, their Table 3 reports a best resolution rate of
23.94\% for GPT-5.3 Codex with oracle summaries. Supermemory's 55.95\%
in their Table 4 is a test-level FAIL\_TO\_PASS rate, not task resolution.
LangMem has retrieval-quality results in their Table 5 but no resolved rate
in their Table 4.
